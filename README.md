# AI-Based Job Matching and Skill Gap Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This system helps job seekers find relevant job opportunities based on their CV/resume and identifies skill gaps between their current qualifications and job requirements. It also provides personalized learning plans to help candidates bridge those gaps.

The platform supports three user roles:
- **Job Seekers** – Upload CVs, browse matched jobs, and receive learning recommendations
- **Employers** – Post job vacancies and find suitable candidates
- **Admins** – Manage users, jobs, and platform activity

---

## 🚀 Features

### For Job Seekers
- Register/Login to your account
- Upload CV (PDF/text) for automatic job matching
- View recommended jobs based on your skills and experience
- Apply to jobs directly through the platform
- Receive AI-generated learning plans to fill skill gaps
- View application history and messages from employers

### For Employers
- Register as an employer account
- Post new job openings
- View applicants who matched with your jobs
- Send messages to potential candidates

### For Admins
- Oversee platform users and job postings
- Manage reported issues

### Technical Features
- CV text extraction and parsing
- Industry classification (Software, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Textile, Agricultural)
- Job matching algorithm based on keyword similarity
- Learning plan generation using AI
